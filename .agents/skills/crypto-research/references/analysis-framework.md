# Analysis Framework

Use this after gathering data to evaluate the token across all relevant
dimensions. Every section feeds into the Green Flags / Red Flags / Risk Rating
in the final report.

---

## Fundamentals

- What problem does it solve? Is there a working product?
- How does it compare to competitors?
- Is the use case sustainable?

**For meme coins / community tokens:** Traditional fundamental analysis doesn't
apply. Instead evaluate:
- Community strength (social media following, engagement, meme virality)
- Whale concentration (even more critical — meme coins live and die by whale movements)
- Exchange listings (more listings = more accessibility and legitimacy)
- Historical pump/dump patterns (has it recovered from previous crashes?)
- Developer activity (is there any, or is it purely speculative?)

---

## Tokenomics

- What % of max supply is circulating?
- Is there inflation or deflation?
- Are there large unlocks coming?
- How concentrated is ownership?
- **For DeFi/RWA tokens:** TVL (Total Value Locked) from CMC — is TVL growing?
  TVL/market cap ratio >1 = potentially undervalued; <0.5 = potentially overvalued

---

## Market Position

- Market cap rank and trajectory
- Volume relative to market cap (healthy turnover?)
- Price trend (accumulation or distribution?)
- **Relative performance vs BTC** — outperforming = showing strength;
  underperforming = losing mindshare

---

## Technical Structure

- RSI zone (oversold / neutral / overbought)
- Trend vs 200d EMA (above = bullish; below = bearish; how far = how extreme)
- Bollinger Band position (near lower = oversold zone; near upper = extended)
- Supertrend direction (UP/DOWN and flip recency)
- MACD momentum (expanding or contracting)

---

## Derivatives & Exchange Data

- Funding rate direction and magnitude (see thresholds in SKILL.md)
- Open interest trend relative to price
- Order book liquidity and imbalance
- Bid-ask spread as liquidity proxy

---

## Red Flags

Each flag includes why it matters, not just what it is.

- **Extreme whale concentration (>10% by few addresses):** Large holders can
  dump and crash price instantly
- **Low holder count relative to market cap:** Thin holder base = price easily
  manipulated
- **Declining holder numbers:** Smart money may be exiting while retail holds bags
- **Negative news sentiment:** Ongoing negative coverage often precedes further
  declines
- **Price down >80% from ATH with no recovery:** May indicate fundamental
  problems, not just market cycles
- **Very low trading volume:** Hard to exit positions without significant slippage
- **Funding rate > +0.05% (extreme positive):** Overleveraged longs risk
  liquidation cascade
- **Funding rate < -0.05% (extreme negative):** Heavy panic shorting, volatile
  environment
- **Rapidly declining open interest:** Traders exiting positions, reduced
  conviction
- **Price >20% below 200d EMA and falling:** Strong bearish trend,
  knife-catching risk
- **Wide bid-ask spread (>0.1%):** Poor liquidity, potential slippage on
  entry/exit

---

## Green Flags

- **Growing holder base:** Organic adoption suggests real demand, not
  manufactured hype
- **High % of long-term holders:** Conviction from holders who have done
  research, less sell pressure
- **Healthy distribution across address sizes:** Resilient to single-actor
  price manipulation
- **Active development and news flow:** Team is shipping, project is alive and
  evolving
- **Strong community engagement:** Network effects build value and create
  sustainable demand
- **Funding rate between -0.01% and +0.01%:** Balanced market without extreme
  leverage
- **Rising open interest with rising price:** Strong trend confirmation from
  new positions entering
- **Price above 200d EMA:** Long-term uptrend intact
- **Tight bid-ask spread (<0.05%):** Deep liquidity, easy entry/exit
- **Outperforming BTC over 7d/30d:** Relative strength, capital rotating in
